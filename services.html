Energy modelling services

Detailed energy consumption forecasts to support sustainable development and efficient building operation. Priced per scheme — send the drawings and we will quote it.

How a job runs

  1. You send the drawings

    Floor plans, elevations, and the glazing and ventilation strategy if you have settled on one. A PDF set is fine.

  2. You get a price and a date

    Fixed, before any work starts, with the method we intend to use and why.

  3. We model it

    To TM59 and Approved Document O, with every assumption written down rather than buried in a software file.

  4. You get the report

    The result, the rooms that drive it, and — if it does not pass — what to change, in the order that will cost you least.
